Quick Start

Step 1

Log in to the admin console and add users who can sign in through SAML.

Step 2

Add a Service Provider using templates for Google or Microsoft, or configure custom SP values.

Step 3

Provide metadata URL and certificate to your SP, then test SSO from the target service.

Operational Notes

Login attempts are monitored for brute-force mitigation. Failed attempts trigger captcha and temporary lockouts.

- Captcha threshold: 3 failed attempts
- Temporary lock: after repeated failures
- Full logs available in Admin > Login Logs
